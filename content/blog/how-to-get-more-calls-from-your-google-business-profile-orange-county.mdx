Most local service businesses in Orange County have a Google Business Profile. Far fewer are actually getting consistent calls from it.

The difference usually isn’t budget. It’s a handful of specific fixes that make Google more confident you should show up in the local pack and Maps when someone nearby searches for what you do.

Here’s what actually moves the needle.

## 1. Complete every relevant field

Google rewards complete profiles. Fill out:
- Primary and secondary categories (be precise)
- Services with descriptions
- Service areas (especially if you travel)
- Business hours (including holiday hours)
- Attributes that match your business

Incomplete profiles lose to complete ones even when the competitor has fewer reviews.

## 2. Get more recent reviews

Reviews still matter, but recency matters more than most owners realize. A steady stream of new reviews signals an active business.

Ask customers right after a job when the experience is fresh. A simple text with a direct link works better than a generic request.

## 3. Post regularly

Google Business Profile posts are not optional if you want visibility. Aim for at least one post per week:
- Recent jobs or before/afters
- Seasonal offers
- Quick tips related to your service
- Clear calls to action (call, book, get a quote)

## 4. Match your website and citations

Your website, Google Business Profile, and other directories should all say the same thing about your name, address, and phone number. Inconsistencies hurt local rankings.

## 5. Make it easy to call

Your phone number should be prominent on your website and clickable on mobile. Track which calls come from Google so you know what’s working.
